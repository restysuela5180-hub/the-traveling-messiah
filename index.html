The Mission

A mission that travels beyond places, boundaries, and screens.

The Traveling Messiah is a missionary journey inspired by Jesus Christ — the Messiah who walked among people, served them, taught them, healed them, and brought hope to those who were searching for God.

The 2TM Project seeks to continue that journey through words, actions, service, technology, travel, and encounters with people.

"The journey belongs to Him. I am His good and humble servant."

From the streets to the digital world, the mission is simple: bring Christ closer to people.

The Journey

Every mission begins with a journey.

The Traveling Messiah began as a calling carried from childhood and slowly became clearer through the experiences of life.


Through struggles, work, service, faith, community, and encounters with people, the journey continued to take shape.


Today, that journey continues through the 2TM Project.
